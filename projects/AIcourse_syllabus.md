## Overview

**ITM TBD: Applied Artificial Intelligence for Business Implementation** is a graduate elective course curriculum that I developed for MIS and MSIS students at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, Shidler College of Business. I designed the course structure, learning outcomes, module sequence, and full syllabus to support graduate-level AI literacy focused on real organizational implementation.

The course builds student judgment and strategic thinking for applying AI effectively in real business contexts. Rather than treating AI as a purely technical subject, it emphasizes how to evaluate AI opportunities, understand system requirements, and implement AI responsibly within modern organizations.

Students move beyond surface-level tool usage to approach AI as a business capability shaped by data quality, governance, risk, and long-term strategy. The curriculum is designed to help future analysts, consultants, and managers understand not only how AI works, but how to deploy it in ways that are scalable, ethical, and measurable.

As organizations continue to adopt AI, many struggle to translate its potential into meaningful outcomes. This course prepares students to bridge that gap by evaluating AI opportunities, designing data-driven solutions, and implementing systems that are both effective and responsible.

## Course Objectives

Students who complete this course will be able to:

- **Align AI with business goals** — Evaluate and communicate how AI initiatives connect to operational workflows, financial outcomes, and organizational transformation
- **Explain core AI concepts** — Articulate machine learning, neural networks, tokenization, and agentic AI with an emphasis on business application
- **Apply real-world case studies** — Analyze documented AI implementations, cloud-based tools, and measurable ROI outcomes from industry
- **Manage data requirements** — Identify data labeling, taxonomy design, pipelines, feature engineering, vector databases, and synthetic data needs for AI projects
- **Develop AI governance** — Create organizational AI policies covering acceptable use, risk management, data stewardship, and regulatory compliance

## Impact

This project reflects a growing need in graduate business education for professionals who can lead AI adoption — not just understand it. By developing this curriculum, I created a structured pathway that integrates technical literacy, implementation strategy, and responsible governance into a single graduate-level course.

The course framework emphasizes long-term organizational value, measurable ROI, and risk-aware decision-making, preparing students to contribute meaningfully to AI initiatives across industries.
